# Tracker 36 — unified minimal redesign

Applied to Live, match workspace, analytics, reports, player activity and personal trainings.

## Visual system
- White primary background across phone, tablet and desktop.
- Sportoteka green `#00A750` retained for active states and primary actions.
- Graphite text with neutral gray secondary text.
- Thin separators instead of heavy card borders.
- Smaller radii: 14 px cards, 10 px inner surfaces, 11 px buttons.
- Reduced shadows and large gray surfaces.
- Compact spacing to preserve more room for maps, charts, players and live data.

## Behavior preserved
- BLE tracker and Polar logic.
- Live session start/pause/stop.
- Session filters and analytics data.
- Reports, exports and player filters.
- Personal training flow and API calls.
